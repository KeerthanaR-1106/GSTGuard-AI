How the Anomaly Index Is Calculated

GSTGuard AI uses an unsupervised ensemble of Isolation Forest (tree partition isolation), Local Outlier Factor (local density anomalies), and Z-Score Analysis (statistical variance across 43 engineered features). Batch-relative detector scores are normalized and combined into a screening index (0–100). This is not a fraud probability, legal finding, or cross-batch comparable score.

0–39 Low Risk Clean, compliant transactions
40–69 Medium Risk Recommended officer verification
≥ 70 High Anomaly Risk Prioritize human review; verify source documents before any compliance decision
